# Ecosystem implementation audit

148 mechanics regressions passed, plus 243 seeded campaigns and 288 paired final waves. Browser QA checked Workshop choice/purchase, retained growth and path, portrait layout, deployment, permanent-loss notice, and live card modal. This is not a formal proof or a fresh full manual campaign.

## Corrected and verified

- Blood Heir/Progenitor have zero attack-growth gauge: permanent self-growth is meals only.
- Nanite and Asura unfinished critical-growth counters persist across battles.
- Named species and ancestral genre enforce predator diets for originals and tokens.
- Breeding rolls once per eligible unit per second, not per neighbor; tokens breed and snapshot stats.
- No per-parent or battle summon count cap; no overlapping slots, maximum nine active units.
- Original deletion survives saves, recovery creates tokens only, original/token rewards match.
- Three food chains have different consumption effects, and cross-genre reactions are explicit.
- Catalyst and Workshop evolution retain identity/growth; purchases validate phase, choice, health and replay.
- Late-card charge reactions have cooldowns and exclude recursive spell feedback.
- Railgun field ATK includes borrowed growth; armor penetration and payoff damage are real.
- Reaper transfer preserves Bleed expiry and attribution; Herald only earns charge for actual allied displacement.
- One canonical rules field serves draft, codex and modal; combination advice is separate.

## Retained limits

The nine-slot board, printed timers, independent DoT expiry and Progenitor healing allowance remain. Growth and DoT stack counts are uncapped. Tokens do not join the roster or inherit permanent-growth engines. Medium may hold one pending echo, but any number of active echoes can occupy free slots. Existing maps require a fresh run to include Workshops. No phone-reset investigation.
