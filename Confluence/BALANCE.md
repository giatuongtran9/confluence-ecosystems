# Food-chain and late-payoff balance

## Seeded campaigns

243 campaigns, nine draft priorities × nine leaders × three seeds; no legacy upgrades. Strategies use normal random offers and purchase an affordable Workshop upgrade. They do not guarantee an ideal ecosystem. Combat minutes exclude player decision time.

| Priority | Wins / 27 | Reached boss | Average combat minutes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| growth | 14 | 16 | 11.8 |
| machines | 23 | 25 | 12.2 |
| volleys | 22 | 24 | 11.9 |
| bleed | 20 | 23 | 11.6 |
| spells | 21 | 24 | 11.9 |
| predation | 22 | 26 | 12 |
| burn | 19 | 22 | 12.8 |
| haunted | 22 | 27 | 12.6 |
| sacrifice | 9 | 15 | 12.2 |

Haunted sacrifice is still harder to assemble than machine/spell decks. Its Lamb Soulflare and true-damage harvest improved the initial sample from 6 to 9 wins; this is a tuning result, not evidence of equal strength.

## Paired final-wave tests

Same five-card ecosystem (30 permanent ATK each), same leader and seed: sixth slot contains either an established low-tier unit with +60 permanent ATK or a fresh late card. Twelve trials per version per scenario; 288 final waves. These are scenario-specific comparisons, not campaign win probabilities.

| Fresh late card | Established alternative | Alternative wins / 12 | Fresh-card wins / 12 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Vector Railgun | Nanite Swarm | 4 | 12 |
| Rune Spellweaver | Jade Disciple | 0 | 12 |
| Storm Archmage | Jade Disciple | 0 | 12 |
| Doomsday Behemoth | Scrap Autocannon | 4 | 12 |
| Singularity Core | Nanite Swarm | 4 | 12 |
| Dark Executioner | Crypt Leech | 10 | 10 |
| Void Eye Obelisk | Soul-Wailing Bell | 0 | 4 |
| Abyssal Reaper | Crypt Leech | 12 | 12 |
| Azathoth Herald | Soul-Wailing Bell | 12 | 12 |
| Veil Medium | Restless Spirit | 4 | 12 |
| Scarlet Matriarch | Scarlet Familiar | 6 | 7 |
| Well of Departed Souls | Grave Lantern | 0 | 10 |

Medium’s own damage excludes its echo damage; support benefits also appear in allied rows. Fresh high-tier cards need not dominate every established carry. Executioner matched its comparison in this sample, whereas Railgun, spellcasters, Behemoth, Core, Medium and Soul Well rescued several otherwise losing formations.
