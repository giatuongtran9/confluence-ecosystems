# Orbital Xeno-Lab & CRISPR Gene-Splicing Ecosystem

## Overview
- **Genre**: Science Fiction (`genre: "SciFi"`)
- **Leader**: Orion, Deep Observer (+30% Critical Hit Damage, -5 Percentage Points Base Critical Hit Chance)
- **Core Mechanics**:
  1. **Biometric Weakpoint Scanning**: Telemetry Sensor, Bio-Scanner Drone, and Optic Drone apply uncapped Weakpoints to runners. Each stack grants +5 percentage points (+0.05) critical hit chance.
  2. **Uncapped Sci-Fi Crits & Conversion**: Non-Sci-Fi units cap at 100% crit chance. Sci-Fi units do NOT cap at 100%; any crit chance exceeding 100% is converted into bonus critical hit damage at a 2:1 ratio (2% excess crit = +1% crit damage).
  3. **Nanite DNA Biopsy & Gene-Splicing**: Focused Nanites extract Chitin, Acid, or Psionic Helix tokens every 6 crits and on scanned priority kills. Gene-Splicer consumes adjacent Helices every 5 seconds to grant +12 Genome and +3 permanent ATK strictly to the deployed Specimen, forging matching Serums into adjacent slots.
  4. **Metamorphosis Progression**:
     - 25 Genome: Evolves to Larva based on first strain (Spine Lurker, Bile Spitter, Optic Drone).
     - 65 Genome: Evolves to Epic Morph based on strain pair (Impaler, Acid Brood, Synapse Nexus, Ravager, Stalker, Siphon), forging a matching Serum.
     - 130 Genome: Mythic Awakening triggered when placed orthogonally adjacent to the matching forged Serum (no Crossing gate required), consuming the Serum to awaken the Mythic Xeno-Leviathan.
  5. **12 Distinct Combat Behaviors**: Complete coverage and testing across all 6 Epic Morphs and 6 Mythic Leviathans.
  6. **Telemetry & Railgun Synergy**: Vector Railgun transfers 2.0x highest growth normally, increasing to 2.5x when Impaler Apex is deployed.
